# Internal Mark Calculator (KTU 2019 scheme)

A simple web page to work out internal marks out of 50 for Vimal Jyothi Engineering College, Chemperi.

- Attendance: percentage divided by 10, out of 10
- Assignment: out of 15
- Assessments: three tests, each out of 60. The best is scaled to 10, the next to 8 and the lowest to 7, giving 25

There is also an assessment only tab that shows the marks out of 25.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step needed.
